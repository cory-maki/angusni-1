Angus Ni

Securities Disputes & Arbitration Counsel

Angus Ni is a New York trial lawyer and co-founder of Morrow Ni LLP who represents individuals and companies in securities disputes, FINRA and international arbitration, and cross-border commercial litigation. He works in English and Mandarin, and many of his clients are Chinese businesses and investors facing proceedings in U.S. courts and tribunals.

About

Institutional training, applied to transnational disputes

Before co-founding Morrow Ni LLP, Angus Ni prosecuted securities class actions at Bernstein Litowitz Berger & Grossmann LLP on behalf of pension fund and hedge fund investors. Earlier, in the litigation department of Debevoise & Plimpton LLP, he worked on ICC and ICSID arbitrations and large-scale corporate investigations. He holds a J.D. with honors from the University of Chicago Law School.

That training now runs in the other direction. At the firm, which practices as Moni Law, Angus represents clients on the receiving end of high-value claims, as in the J.P. Morgan FINRA arbitration, where a six-day hearing ended with the claim cut from more than $6.4 million to $279,250.

Mr. Ni also served as pro bono trial counsel in United States v. Zhu, a federal criminal case that ended in a judgment of acquittal. His professional background in full covers that training, his matters and his involvement in New York’s Chinese-American legal community. Prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome.
